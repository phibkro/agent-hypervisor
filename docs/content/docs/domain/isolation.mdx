## Sandbox

A sandbox is a Cloud Hypervisor microVM. Stopping it keeps nothing in memory: the worktree, the harness transcript
and the record live on the host, so resuming boots a fresh VM and the harness reloads its session. Snapshots are a
later optimisation; restoring VMs with virtio-fs mounts has open upstream issues.

The idle rule lives here: after 15 minutes without activity from the owner or any agent, the sandbox stops,
unless a request is pending.

## Mounts follow capabilities

A directory capability becomes a virtio-fs mount, hot-plugged when granted and removed when revoked. The
[formal model](/docs/formal-model) showed this must happen in the same step as any loss of authority (revoking,
and also freezing iso into val), not lazily afterwards.
